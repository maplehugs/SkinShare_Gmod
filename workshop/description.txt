[h1]🎭 SkinShare — Better Skin Selector[/h1]
[h3]Type [b]!skin[/b]. Pick ANY playermodel you own. Everyone sees it. Instantly.[/h3]

[quote]No reconnect. No server collection. No admin. Made for playing with your friends: anyone can pick any skin they have and send it to everyone else.[/quote]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]😤 The problem[/h2]
You join your friend's game and want to use your favorite playermodel... but the host doesn't have it. Your friends see you as a giant red [b]ERROR[/b], or the selector doesn't even list your skins.

[h2]✅ The fix[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]🌐 Built for peer-to-peer.[/b] Host from the main menu like always. [u]Only the host needs this addon[/u]; friends get it automatically when they join. Dedicated servers work too.
[*][b]👤 YOUR skins, not just the host's.[/b] The menu finds playermodels in your own subscribed addons, even ones nobody else has.
[*][b]📦 Auto-download for everyone.[/b] When you pick a skin, every other player downloads and mounts it mid-game. It's only applied once they have it, so nobody sees an ERROR.
[*][b]🔪 Works in TTT and any gamemode.[/b] Your skin is forced over the gamemode's model and survives respawns and new rounds. Prop Hunt props are left alone.
[*][b]💾 Persistent.[/b] Your last skin is saved on your PC and put back on whenever you join, even after restarting GMod or playing another day.
[/list]

[h2]✨ Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]🧍 Live 3D preview: drag to rotate, scroll to zoom
[*]🔍 Searchable icon grid of every playermodel you have
[*]🧩 Bodygroups and skins, 😉 face (flexes), 🎨 player and weapon color
[*]⭐ Favorites: save and load complete setups
[*]✋ Matching first-person hands when the addon ships them
[*]🌍 English and Spanish: follows your GMod language, or use the EN/ES button
[/list]

[h2]🚀 How to use[/h2]
[olist]
[*]The host subscribes and hosts a game (P2P or dedicated).
[*]Friends join. Nothing to install.
[*]Everyone types [b]!skin[/b], picks a model and hits [b]Use and share[/b].
[*]That's it. 🎉
[/olist]

[h2]⚠️ Notes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Shared skins must be public Workshop items tagged "Model" (up to 300 MB), so nobody can force everyone to mount texture or sound replacement packs.
[*]If you also use Enhanced PlayerModel Selector, set [b]sv_playermodel_selector_force 0[/b]. Its force mode overrides every model change.
[/list]
