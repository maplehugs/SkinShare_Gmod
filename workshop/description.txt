[h1]Your skins. Their screens. No reconnect.[/h1]

[b]Ever joined a friend's game and showed up as a giant red ERROR?[/b] Or opened the playermodel menu and found none of your skins, only the host's? Tired of asking the host to add your model to the collection and restart?

[b]SkinShare fixes all of that.[/b] Type [b]!skin[/b] in chat, pick any playermodel YOU own, and every other player downloads it and sees it on you [b]mid-game[/b]. No server collection, no admin and no reconnect.

Made for playing with friends: [b]anyone[/b] in the game can pick [b]any[/b] skin they have and send it to everyone else.

[hr][/hr]

[h1]Features[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Built for peer-to-peer.[/b] Host from the main menu like always. Only the host needs this addon; friends get it automatically when they join. Dedicated servers work too.
[*][b]Use YOUR skins on anyone's game.[/b] The menu lists playermodels from your own subscribed addons, even ones the host has never seen.
[*][b]Everyone sees you, not an ERROR.[/b] Other players download your skin from the Workshop on the fly, and it's only applied once they've mounted it.
[*][b]Works in every gamemode.[/b] TTT, Sandbox, Murder and more. Your skin is enforced over the gamemode's model and stays through respawns and new rounds. Non-playermodels (like Prop Hunt props) are left alone.
[*][b]Remembers you.[/b] Your last skin is saved on your PC and reapplied every time you join, even after restarting GMod or coming back another day.
[*][b]Full customization:[/b]
[list]
[*]Live 3D preview: drag to rotate, scroll to zoom
[*]Searchable icon grid of every playermodel you have
[*]Skins and bodygroups
[*]Face (flexes)
[*]Player and weapon color
[*]Viewmodel hands (when the addon includes them)
[*]Favorites: right-click any model or the preview to save the full setup; click to load, right-click to rename, overwrite or delete
[/list]
[*][b]Admin panel[/b] (only admins see it): turn SkinShare on/off, admin-only mode, cooldown, max addon size, block gamemodes (e.g. Murder, Prop Hunt) or specific addons, and a player list to remove someone's skin, block their addon or ban them from SkinShare.
[*][b]Extras:[/b] random model button, copy model path, rescan models without changing map.
[*][b]English and Spanish.[/b] Follows your GMod language, or switch with the EN/ES button.
[/list]

[h1]How to use[/h1]
[olist]
[*]The host subscribes to SkinShare and starts a game.
[*]Friends join. They don't need to install anything.
[*]Type [b]!skin[/b] in chat, [b]skinshare[/b] in the console, or click the SkinShare icon in the Sandbox C menu.
[*]Pick a model, tweak it, hit [b]Use and share[/b]. Done.
[/olist]

[h1]Commands[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]!skin[/b] / [b]skinshare[/b]: open the menu
[*][b]skinshare_fav "name"[/b]: use a favorite without opening the menu (bind it to a key!)
[*][b]skinshare_random[/b]: use a random favorite
[*][b]skinshare_language auto | en | es[/b]: menu language
[*][b]skinshare_debug[/b]: list which of your addons the menu can see
[/list]

[h1]Server settings[/h1]
Everything is in the Admin tab, or use these from the server console:
[list]
[*][b]sv_skinshare_enabled 1[/b] / [b]sv_skinshare_adminonly 0[/b]
[*][b]sv_skinshare_cooldown 1[/b]: seconds between changes (admins skip it)
[*][b]sv_skinshare_maxsize 300[/b]: max size in MB of a shared addon
[*][b]sv_skinshare_block_gamemodes "murder,prop_hunt"[/b]
[*][b]sv_skinshare_block_addons "123,456"[/b]: Workshop IDs that can't be shared
[*][b]skinshare_admin strip|ban|unban <name or SteamID>[/b]
[/list]
[b]For developers:[/b] return false from the [b]SkinShareCanUse[/b] hook (shared) to block SkinShare for a player, e.g. during a round or for a team.

[h1]FAQ[/h1]
[b]Do my friends need to subscribe?[/b]
No. Only the host does. The menu is sent to everyone when they join.

[b]My skin doesn't show up in the list.[/b]
Make sure the addon is enabled in your Addons menu, then run [b]skinshare_debug[/b] in the console and check that it's listed.

[b]Can anyone make everyone download anything?[/b]
No. Only public Workshop items tagged "Model" and up to 300 MB (configurable) are shared, and admins can block any addon. Texture or sound replacement packs can't be pushed on other players.

[b]Does it work with Enhanced PlayerModel Selector?[/b]
Yes, but set [b]sv_playermodel_selector_force 0[/b]. Otherwise its force mode overrides every model change, including SkinShare's.
