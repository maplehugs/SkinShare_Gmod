[h1]SkinShare - Better Skin Selector[/h1]
[b]Pick any playermodel you own, from chat, on anyone's server, and everyone else sees it. No reconnect, no server collection, no admin needed.[/b]

Type [b]!skin[/b] in chat. A menu opens with a live 3D preview of every playermodel from YOUR subscribed addons. Pick one, hit [b]Use and share[/b], and every other player downloads and mounts it mid-game.

[h2]Why this one?[/h2]
Normal selectors only list models the [i]server[/i] has, so your friends end up as ERRORs or can't use their own skins at all. SkinShare fixes that:
[list]
[*][b]Made for peer-to-peer / listen servers.[/b] Host a game from the main menu with your friends: only the host needs this addon, and clients get it automatically when they join. Dedicated servers work too.
[*][b]Your skins, not just the host's.[/b] The menu finds playermodels in your own addons, even ones the host has never seen.
[*][b]Everyone sees you.[/b] Other players download your skin from the Workshop on the fly, and it's only applied once they have it, so nobody sees an ERROR.
[*][b]Works in any gamemode.[/b] TTT, Sandbox, Murder and more. Your skin is forced over the gamemode's model and stays through respawns and new rounds. Non-playermodels (like Prop Hunt props) are left alone.
[/list]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Live 3D preview: drag to rotate, scroll to zoom
[*]Searchable icon grid of every playermodel you have
[*]Bodygroups and skins, face (flexes), and player and weapon color
[*]Favorites: save and load full setups
[*]Remembers your last skin and puts it back on when you join
[*]Matching first-person hands when the addon ships them
[*]English and Spanish: follows your GMod language, or use the EN/ES button
[/list]

[h2]Commands[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]!skin[/b] or [b]skinshare[/b]: open the menu
[*][b]skinshare_language auto / en / es[/b]: menu language
[*][b]skinshare_debug[/b]: list which of your addons the menu can see
[/list]

[h2]Notes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Shared skins must be public Workshop items tagged "Model" (up to 300 MB). Nobody can force everyone to mount texture or sound replacement packs.
[*]If you also use Enhanced PlayerModel Selector, set [b]sv_playermodel_selector_force 0[/b]. Its force mode overrides every model change.
[/list]

[h2]Credits[/h2]
The menu layout is inspired by [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=504945881]Enhanced PlayerModel Selector[/url] by LibertyForce. SkinShare is a separate implementation that adds cross-player skin sharing.
