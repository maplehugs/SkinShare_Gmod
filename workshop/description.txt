[h1]SkinShare - Better Skin Selector[/h1]
Type [b]!skin[/b] in chat to pick a playermodel with a live 3D preview. Your friends download and mount it [b]mid-game[/b], with no reconnect and no need to add it to the server's collection.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Use YOUR skins on anyone's server[/b]: the menu shows playermodels from your own subscribed addons, even when the host doesn't have them. Everyone else downloads them automatically.
[*]Works on [b]peer-to-peer / listen servers[/b] and dedicated servers. Only the host needs this addon; clients get it on join.
[*]Works in any gamemode ([b]TTT[/b], Sandbox, ...). Your skin is forced over the gamemode's model and survives respawns and new rounds. Non-playermodels (Prop Hunt props) are left alone.
[*]Tabs for bodygroups and skin, face (flexes), player and weapon color, and favorites.
[*]Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom.
[*]Remembers your last skin and reapplies it when you join.
[*]Matching viewmodel hands when the addon ships them.
[/list]

[h2]Commands[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]!skin[/b] or [b]skinshare[/b]: open the menu
[*][b]skinshare_debug[/b]: list which of your addons the menu can see
[/list]

[h2]Notes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Only public Workshop items tagged "Model" (up to 300 MB) are shared, so nobody can force everyone to mount texture or sound replacement packs.
[*]Not compatible with Enhanced PlayerModel Selector's force mode. Either set [b]sv_playermodel_selector_force 0[/b] or disable that addon.
[/list]
